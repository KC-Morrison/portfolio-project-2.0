How It Works

Use pxRect(x, y, width, height, color) to draw rectangles.

Colors available:

GB.darkest
GB.dark
GB.light
GB.lightest

Tips:

  • x and y are your starting point (already set up)
  • Add to x to move right, add to y to move down
  • Draw dark outlines first, then lighter fills on top
  • Keep shapes small (under 20 pixels) for pixel art look
